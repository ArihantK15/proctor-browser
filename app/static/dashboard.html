Per-Student Randomization

Give every student their own deterministic question order and option layout. Two students sitting side by side see different sequences, so "the answer is B" doesn't help a neighbour. Same student on a resume sees the exact same view.

Remove Student From Roster

Removes a student's enrollment under your account so they can re-register cleanly. Their login account is kept; only the roster row is deleted. A roster entry spans all your exams, so removal applies to all of them.

Clear Sessions

Destructive. Wipes stale in-progress exam sessions for your account — along with the saved answers, violation events and screenshots that belong to them. A student whose client has sent a heartbeat in the last 2 minutes is considered actively taking the exam and is never touched. Your questions and other teachers' data are never touched. Requires two confirmations.
