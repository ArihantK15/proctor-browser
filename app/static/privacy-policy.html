Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 1, 2026

Procta provides AI-proctored exam services to educational institutions. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your personal data.

1. Information We Collect

Account Information: Name, email address, phone number, roll number, institution name.

Exam Data: Exam answers, screen recordings, camera feeds (laptop and phone), audio recordings, keystroke patterns, mouse movements.

Device Information: Browser type, operating system, IP address, display configuration, connected peripherals.

Violation Data: Automated integrity flags (gaze tracking, face presence, multiple faces, screen sharing, VPN/proxy detection).

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To verify student identity before exams
  • To detect and prevent academic dishonesty during exams
  • To generate exam scores and performance analytics
  • To communicate exam invitations, reminders, and results
  • To provide teacher dashboards and review tools
  • To improve our AI models (anonymized and aggregated data only)

3. Data Sharing

We share data only with:

  • Your educational institution — exam results, violation reports, session recordings
  • Service providers — Supabase (database), Groq/LLM providers (AI grading), Razorpay (payments)
  • Law enforcement — when required by applicable law

We never sell your personal data. We never use exam content for training unrelated AI models.

4. Data Retention

  • Screenshots: 90 days, then permanently deleted
  • Phone camera frames: 24 hours, then permanently deleted
  • Exam answers and scores: Retained for the duration of your account
  • Violation logs and audit trails: 1 year

5. Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access your personal data — use the Privacy Center to export your data
  • Rectify inaccurate data — update your profile in the dashboard
  • Delete your account and associated data — use the Privacy Center
  • Object to processing — contact your institution's exam administrator
  • Data portability — export your data as JSON from the Privacy Center

Exercise these rights at /privacy or by contacting support@procta.net.

6. Security

We implement encryption at rest (AES-256) and in transit (TLS 1.3), regular security audits, access controls, and employee confidentiality agreements. See our Security Questionnaire for details.
